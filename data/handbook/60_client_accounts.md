# Section 6 · Client & Industry Notes

Kalam handles calls for many clients across industries. These notes adjust how records are handled.

**Clause 6.1 — Healthcare clients.** Any clinical detail, appointment, or health concern is sensitive (Clause 4.3) and routed to review. Never auto-save a record that contains a health detail.

**Clause 6.2 — Banking & financial clients.** Disputed charges, suspected fraud, and account-security calls are High or Urgent and, where a trigger applies, escalated. Identity must be verified before any account change is documented as done (Clause 4.4).

**Clause 6.3 — Retail & e-commerce clients.** Returns, refunds, and order issues are routine and usually auto-saveable when complete. A refund actually processed on the call is Resolved; a refund promised for later is Unresolved — pending.

**Clause 6.4 — Government & public-sector clients.** Records must be especially precise and are held to the strictest grounding standard; anything ambiguous is routed to review.

**Clause 6.5 — Which client a call belongs to.** The client and the customer's history are looked up from the CRM records for the account on the call; do not assume the industry from the topic alone.
