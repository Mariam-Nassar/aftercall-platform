# Section 1 · Category & Subcategory Taxonomy

Records must be classified into one of these categories. Do not invent categories outside this list; if nothing fits, use "Other" and route to review.

**Clause 1.1 — Billing & Payments.** Subcategories: incorrect charge, payment failed, invoice request, billing dispute. A *billing dispute* is the customer contesting a charge they believe is wrong; it stays in Billing.

**Clause 1.2 — Returns & Refunds.** Subcategories: refund request, return status, refund not received. A *refund request* asks for money back for a product/service; it is distinct from a Billing dispute (which contests a charge). Classify by what the customer is asking for: money back for a purchase → Returns & Refunds; "this charge is wrong" → Billing.

**Clause 1.3 — Order & Delivery.** Subcategories: order status, delivery delay, wrong or damaged item, missing item.

**Clause 1.4 — Technical Support.** Subcategories: cannot log in, service outage, device or app fault, configuration help.

**Clause 1.5 — Account Management.** Subcategories: update details, add or remove service, upgrade or downgrade, security concern.

**Clause 1.6 — Cancellation.** Subcategories: cancel service, cancel order, retention. A *cancellation* is distinct from a *complaint*: wanting to leave is a cancellation even if the customer is unhappy.

**Clause 1.7 — Complaint.** Subcategories: service quality, staff conduct, repeated failure. A complaint expresses dissatisfaction; it is not automatically an escalation (see Section 4 for escalation triggers).

**Clause 1.8 — Product & Service Inquiry.** Subcategories: pre-sale question, how-to, availability, pricing.

**Clause 1.9 — Appointments & Bookings.** Subcategories: book, reschedule, cancel appointment, confirm.

**Clause 1.10 — Other.** Anything that does not fit the above. Records classified "Other" are always routed to review.
