# Section 3 · Sentiment

**Clause 3.1 — Sentiment values.** Record one of: Positive, Neutral, Negative, At-risk.

**Clause 3.2 — Reading sentiment.**
Judge the customer's sentiment from their words and tone across the call, not from a single line.
- **Positive** — the customer expresses satisfaction, thanks, or goodwill.
- **Neutral** — a routine, transactional call with no strong feeling either way.
- **Negative** — the customer is frustrated, annoyed, or disappointed, but still engaging.
- **At-risk** — the customer signals they may leave, escalate externally, or take legal or public action ("I'm cancelling," "I'll go to the regulator," "I'll post about this"). At-risk sentiment is an escalation trigger (Section 4).

**Clause 3.3 — Sentiment is not disposition.**
An angry customer whose issue was fixed is a Negative (or recovering) sentiment with a Resolved disposition. A calm customer whose issue was not fixed is Neutral sentiment with an Unresolved disposition. Keep the two judgements separate.
